# SOP-001 — GxP Document Control

PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for creation, review, approval,
distribution, retrieval, revision, retirement, and archival of GxP controlled documents (i.e., SOPs, Work
Instructions, Policies, Manuals, Forms, and Templates, etc.) at Netramind.
SCOPE
This SOP applies to all employees and contractors involved in the creation of new and revised
controlled documents generated at Netramind.
REFERENCES
• SOP-002 Good Documentation Practices
• SOP-004 GxP Records Retention
• SOP-003 GxP Training Program and Curricula
DEFINITIONS
1.1 Administrative Change—Change to a controlled document that does not affect the content or
process (ex: grammar, formatting, typographical errors, and organizational name changes).
1.2 Change Control — The process of reviewing proposed changes for impact to quality, study
participant safety, and regulatory compliance in a documented manner.
1.3 Change Request — A formal means to document Change Control.
1.4 Controlled Copy—A formal copy of the current version of an approved, controlled document.
When changes are needed, the controlled copy is retrieved and replaced with the most updated
version. A controlled copy must be systematically tracked, updated, and stored.
1.5 Controlled Document— Documents (i.e., Quality Manual, SOPs, Work Instructions [WIs], Forms
[FMs], etc.) required by the Quality Management System (QMS) that are controlled throughout
the document lifecycle by identification, storage, protection, version control, retrieval, retention,
and disposition.
1.6 Document Control — The process of controlling documentation throughout its lifecycle; this
includes all tracking, review, approval, versioning, and obsoletion (as required) of controlled
documents.
1.7 Document Number—A unique ID number assigned to controlled documents and their revisions.
1.8 Effective Date— The date upon which a controlled document is considered to take effect (i.e.,
issued). This date is based on the QA approval signature date of each controlled document. QA
and author ensure that the team is trained between the approval and effective dates.
1.9 GxP—A collection of quality guidelines and regulations for the life sciences industry created to
ensure that pharmaceutical drug products are safe, of acceptable quality, meet their intended
use, and adhere to quality processes and systems during manufacturing, packaging/labeling,
testing, holding and distribution. The term GxP is a general abbreviation for good practice
guidelines and regulations which collectively cover good laboratory practices (GLP), good clinical
practices (GCP), good pharmacovigilance practices (GPV), good manufacturing practices (GMP)
and good distribution practices (GDP).
1.10Form—A type of controlled document used to capture information while performing steps in an
SOP or WI. Users cannot edit information fields or formatting in forms.
1.11Master Document List—The list of cross-functional Netramind controlled documents maintained
by Document Management.
1.12Metadata – The contextual information required to understand a given data element. Metadata
is structured information that describes, explains or otherwise makes it easier to retrieve, use or
manage data. For the purpose of this guideline, relevant metadata are those needed to allow the
appropriate evaluation of the trial conduct.
1.13Obsolete Document—A document version that has been superseded by a revision.
1.14Periodic Review—Regularly scheduled, documented review of controlled documents, ensuring
continued applicability, usability, and compliance with applicable regulatory requirements.
1.15Quality Management System (QMS)—A formalized system that defines and documents
processes, procedures, and responsibilities for achieving quality policies, practices and
objectives.
1.16Retired Document—A controlled document is removed from the system.
1.17Template—A type of controlled document that defines content structure for a specific document
type or deliverable. The use of templates ensures consistency of content and styles while
allowing flexibility in format and required information.
1.18Uncontrolled Copy—A copy of a document whose versions are not monitored after distribution.
Copies of documents made by users are considered uncontrolled. It is the user’s responsibility to
ensure that a document is the most current, approved version prior to use.
1.19Version Number—A sequentially assigned number recorded at the end of a Document Number.
Version numbers are assigned by Document Management.
1.20Work Instruction (WI)—A type of controlled document that provides more in-depth instructions
for how to complete a specific task described in an SOP.
RESPONSIBILITIES
1.21Document Management or Designee
1.21.1 Maintain all controlled documents in the Quality Management System (QMS) in
accordance with SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.
1.21.2 Conduct periodic reviews of controlled documents
1.21.3 Notify personnel of new, revised, or retired controlled documents
1.21.4 Review FRM-001-01 Document Change Request (DCR) Form
1.21.5 Maintain the TMP-001-01 Document Change Request Log.
1.21.6 Manage the archival and retirement of controlled documents
1.22Functional Area (FA) Manager or Designee
1.22.1 Assign Authors to lead the development of functional specific document(s)
1.22.2 Review and approve functional specific controlled documents
1.22.3 Ensure appropriate justification for controlled document creation, revision, or retirement.
1.23Author or Designee
1.23.1 Initiate the creation, review, approval, revision, and/or retirement of controlled
documents within their FA.
1.23.2 Complete FRM-001-01 Document Change Request (DCR) Form
1.23.3 Ensure that controlled documents accurately reflect Netramind business practices and
are compliant with applicable regulatory requirements and/or industry standard
practices.
1.23.4 Collaborate with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to ensure content is current and
accurate.
1.23.5 Identify an appropriate training audience (if applicable), develop training materials,
designate the Training Methodology, and/or conduct applicable training.
1.24Quality Assurance (QA) or Designee
1.24.1 Review documents to ensure that all required steps are present and that the document
aligns with applicable regulatory requirements, regulatory guidance, and/or industry
standard practices.
1.24.2 Review and/or approve final versions of controlled documents.
1.24.3 Collaborate with the Author to determine training requirements.
PROCEDURE
1.25Generating a New or Revising an Existing Controlled Document
1.25.1 Functional Area (FA) Manager assigns an Author to prepare a new or revised document.
1.25.2 The Author completes FRM-001-01 Document Change Request (DCR) Form for new and
revised controlled documents and forwards it to Document Management with, if
applicable, a request for the most current version of the controlled document(s) to be
updated.
1.25.2.1 Document Management reviews the DCR Form and assigns a DCR Number in the
following format [DCR-YY-XXX], where:
• YY - denotes the last two (2) digits of the current year
• XXX – denotes a three-digit sequential tracking number beginning with
001. This number resets each year to begin with 001.
• Ex: DCR-23-001 is assigned to the first document change request in 2023.
• NOTE: If an electronic Document Management system is used, it may
automatically assign the DCR number. Refer to the applicable SOP or
work instruction (WI) which governs system use.
1.25.2.2 Document Management captures change request information in the TMP-001-
01 Document Change Request (DCR) Log.
1.25.2.3 Document Management forwards an editable copy of the controlled
document(s), as applicable.
1.25.3 Document Management maintains the DCR Log that contains the following minimum
information:
• DCR Number
• Document Number (ex: SOP number, WI number, Form number, etc.)
• Document Title
• Current Document Version Number
• Revision Version Number (subsequent number after revision)
• Document Type (Attachment A)
• Initiator
• Document Action (i.e., new, retire, review)
• Status (i.e., complete, ongoing, on hold)
• Effective Date
• Retirement Date
• List of Documents Affected by Changes
• Comments
Note: This can be maintained in an electronic Document Management System, if utilized.
1.25.4 The Author collaborates with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to develop or revise the
document.
1.25.4.1 Document Management assigns a number to the New controlled document
according to Attachment A of this SOP
1.25.4.2 The Author and QA identify reviewers and approvers according to Attachment B
of this SOP.
1.25.4.3 The Author uses the Style Guide (Attachment C) of this SOP.
1.25.4.4 The Author incorporates comments from reviewers in a timely manner.
1.25.5 The Author circulates the clean, updated version to QA for review.
1.25.5.1 QA forwards any feedback to the Author.
1.25.5.2 The Author incorporates feedback and sends it back to QA until all content can
be finalized.
1.25.6 The Author signs and dates the completed/updated controlled document and FRM-001-
01 Document Change Request (DCR) Form and circulates it to the Reviewer and Approver
for their signatures.
1.25.7 When all signatures have been obtained, the Author sends the peer-reviewed controlled
document and FRM-001-01 Document Change Request (DCR) Form to Document
Management.
1.25.7.1 When a new version of a controlled document has been approved, Document
Management marks the previous version “Obsolete.”
1.25.8 QA collaborates with Document Management to assign an Effective Date. Effective Date is
assigned following approval and completion of required training.
1.25.9 If administrative changes (ex: grammatical or typographical errors) are identified after the
document becomes effective, these may be noted in the DCR Log and addressed during
the next revision.
1.26Controlled Document Notification
1.26.1 QA uploads a PDF and/or controlled/protected version of the newly approved or revised,
effective document to the appropriate Shared Drive folder or the Electronic Document
Management System, as applicable, and removes the PDF and/or controlled/protected
version of the retired or obsoleted document from circulation.
1.26.2 Document Management notifies Netramind personnel of new, revised, and retired
controlled documents, including the following information:
• Document Title, Number, and revised Version Number
• FA that owns the document
• Effective or Retirement Date
• Type of Change (Technical, Administrative, or Periodic Review)
• Training Required and Completion date
1.26.3 Netramind employees must complete training within the assigned training period and
ensure that all training documentation is forwarded to Document Management. Refer to
SOP-003 GxP Training Program and Curricula.

1.27.1 Document Management maintains the TMP-001-01 Master List of Controlled Documents
and the periodic review schedule, according to the below:

• POLs • Policies

• Forms

1.27.2 Document Management regularly reviews the Master List of Controlled Documents and
identifies those eligible for periodic review within the next 12 months.
1.27.2.1 When an eligible document is identified, Document Management notifies the
relevant FA Manager approximately 90 calendar days prior to the periodic
review start date.
1.27.2.2 The Author contacts the FA Manager and QA to coordinate the type of action
required (i.e., no change, revision, retirement) within 30 business days; all
correspondence is documented in the QMS.
1.27.2.3 If no changes are required, Document Management assigns the next scheduled
review date according to the table above and records it on the DCR Log.
a. The Version and Document Number remain the same. The Author, Reviewer,
and QA document the decision.
1.27.2.4 If changes are required, perform steps in Section 6.1 of this SOP.
1.27.3 FA Manager monitors Authors to ensure that periodic reviews are conducted in a timely
manner.
1.27.3.1 If there are significant delays or no acknowledgment from the FA Manager, QA
and/or Document Management escalate the issue to Netramind Senior
Management.
1.28Handling of Controlled Document Copies
1.28.1 Printed copies are considered uncontrolled unless specifically designated.
1.28.2 When printed copies of controlled documents are to be shared with third party
consultants, external auditors, or during Regulatory Health Authority Inspections, they
are marked, “Uncontrolled Copy” and tracked by Document Management. Uncontrolled
copies may not be retrieved after use.
1.28.3 Use of ink stamps or electronic watermarks is acceptable to mark both Controlled and
Uncontrolled Copies.
1.29Document Retirement
1.29.1 The Author, Document Management, and QA complete Section 6.1.1 through
Section 6.1.2.2 of this SOP.
1.29.2 The Author and Document Management verify that the controlled document is not
currently in use by any FA.
1.29.3 The responsible FA Manager and QA approve the retirement of the controlled document
and assigns an Effective Retirement date.
1.29.4 Document Management and captures the Retirement Date on the DCR Log.
1.29.5 If a controlled document is maintained as a hard copy, Document Management stamps
each page, “Retired” and signs and dates the first page as acknowledgement of its new
status.
1.29.6 If a controlled document is maintained in an electronic document management system,
Document Management follows applicable steps for removing the document from all
accessible files.
1.29.7 Document Management notifies affected personnel that the document is retired. All
retired controlled documents are maintained in the Document Management System and
archived according to SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.
1.30Controlled Document Repository
1.30.1 Controlled documents are maintained electronically in a secure repository with role-
based access control.
1.30.2 A new version of the Master List of Controlled Documents is created each year and
documents the following minimum information:
• Document Type
• Document Number
• Document Title
• Version Number
• Effective Date
• Last Review Date
• Scheduled Review Date
1.30.3 GxP controlled documents are maintained in the Document Management System and
archived according to SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.

ATTACHMENT C: STYLE GUIDE FOR CONTROLLED DOCUMENTS
When writing documents, use the following as a style guide:
• Paper size: May be either A4 or US Letter 8.5”x11”
• Font Type: Times New Roman, Calibri (or similar font type)
• Font Size: 11pt
• Date format: Refer to SOP-002 Good Documentation Practices
• Numbering:
− Main sections 1., 2., 3. etc.
− Sub-sections 1.1., 1.2., 1.3., etc.
− Sub-sub-sections 1.1.1., 1.1.2., 1.1.3., etc.
− Bullet points may be used for further subsections and/or a list that does not have to be
performed in a sequential order
• Header: Approved [Sponsor] Logo, Document Type, Document Number, Effective Date, Version
Number, “Page _ of _” (optional), and the Document Title.
• Footer: Confidentiality Statement and “Page _ of _”
• Signature Page: Names, Functional Areas (FAs), Signatures, and Dates of the Author, Reviewer, and
Approver
