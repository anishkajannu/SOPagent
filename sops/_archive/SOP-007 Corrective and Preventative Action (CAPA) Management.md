# SOP-007 — Corrective and Preventative Action (CAPA) Management

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for initiating, implementing, tracking
and approving/closing Corrective Actions and Preventive Actions (CAPAs) and verifying the
effectiveness of these actions associated with a noncompliance or non-conformance associated with
the conduct of GxP activities at Netramind.
2 SCOPE
This SOP applies to all Netramind employees, contractors/consultants handling CAPAs resulting from
investigations and deviations arising from a non-conformance related to GxP activities. Additionally,
the tracking and oversight of CAPAs generated by contract service providers (CSPs) on behalf of
Netramind.
This SOP does not apply to protocol deviations arising within a clinical trial conduct.
3 REFERENCES
• SOP-006 Deviation Management
• SOP-016 Data Integrity
• International Conference on Harmonization (ICH) E6 (R3) Good Clinical Practice
4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)— A systematic approach that includes actions needed
to correct (“correction”), prevent recurrence (“corrective action”), and eliminate the cause of
potential nonconforming product and other quality problems (preventive action).
4.2 CAPA Owner—Person assigned the responsibility for the implementation of, or facilitating the
implementation of, CAPA actions based on their combined level of experience, knowledge and
training.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 CAPA Owners
5.1.1 Initiate the CAPA plans and assign CAPA team members.
5.1.2 Lead the deviation/non-conformance investigation to identify root cause(s) for the
reported issue.
5.1.3 Work cross functionally to define tasks, effectiveness checks required for the CAPA plan.
5.1.4 Collaborate with QA to generate and approve a CAPA Plan and monitor requirements for
an Effectiveness Check (if applicable).
5.1.5 Oversee the appropriate execution and completion of the CAPA plan and deliverables.
5.1.6 Oversee the execution and assessment of appropriateness of effectiveness checks.
5.1.7 Providing accurate summaries and supporting evidence of execution is accurate,
complete and traceable to the CAPA
5.2 Quality Assurance (QA)
5.2.1 Ensures CAPAs are documented, tracked, and appropriately closed.
5.3 Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)
5.3.1 Review, approve CAPA plans based on CAPA ownership and impact assessment
5.3.2 Act as a subject matter expert (SME) assessor or approver for CAPA investigation,
planning, review, approval.
5.4 Functional Area Management (FAM)
5.4.1 Provides input and approval, as needed.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 CAPA Management
6.1.1 CAPAs are generated to prevent recurrence of known or suspected root causes of
deviations or other non-conformances such as, but not limited to:
• Deviation and Investigations
• Product complaint investigations
• Regulatory agency inspection observations
• Quality system failures or deficiencies
6.1.2 External CAPAs may be tracked where relevant to Netramind activities
6.1.3 CAPAs and ECs are tracked by QA using the Netramind electronic quality management
system (eQMS) if applicable. eQMS administration and user instructions are provided as
applicable.
6.1.4 QA or the eQMS assigns a CAPA number. If assigning a number manually, the numbering
schema issuing the letters CAPA, followed by the four digits of the current year, followed
by a three-digit sequential number (e.g., CAPA-2025-001).
6.1.5 QA documents the CAPA Plan in the system of record or on FRM-007-01 CAPA Form and
the plan includes:
• List of action(s) required to address the incident.
• List of person(s) responsible for completion of each action.
• Timelines for completion.
• Effectiveness checks requirements.
6.1.6 Target completion dates are assigned based on the scope and urgency of the CAPA.
6.1.7 CAPAs are reviewed and approved as appropriate.
6.2 CAPA Planning and Execution
6.2.1 The CAPA Owner and QA may form a CAPA team with appropriate SMEs to assist with the
CAPA execution.
6.2.2 Additional information from investigation, deviation summaries, and other sources may
be used to complete the CAPA plan targeting the root cause(s) and issues.
6.2.3 The CAPA Owner collaborates with impacted stakeholders, including FAMs, SMEs to
gather all required documentation relevant to the quality event, corrective actions, and
forwards the CAPA documentation package to QA before the CAPA due date.
6.3 Effectiveness Checks (EC) Planning and Execution
6.3.1 Where appropriate, effectiveness of CAPA actions may be assessed to confirm the issue
has been resolved.
6.4 CAPA and EC Review and Approval
6.4.1 The CAPA Owner documents the completion of all prescribed CAPA actions on FRM-007-
01 CAPA Form or in the system of record and attaches all associated documentation and
records related to the execution of the CAPA Plan.
6.4.2 QA reviews the CAPA documentation package to determine if the following have been
completed:
• All actions identified in the CAPA report have been completed.
• Data attachments are complete and signed/approved, as appropriate.
6.4.3 If an Effectiveness Check is not required, QA approves and closes the CAPA.
6.4.4 If an Effectiveness review is required, QA, in conjunction with the CAPA Owner, monitors
the effectiveness of the CAPA per the criteria established in the approved CAPA Plan.
CAPAs can only be closed out completely by QA when the effectiveness checks have
demonstrated successful and acceptable results.
6.4.5 If the implemented CAPA actions were deemed effective, the CAPA record is finalized to
show all associated CAPA actions are now closed.
6.4.6 If the associated CAPA actions were not effective or were discovered to have negative
impact post-implementation, the CAPA and the associated effectiveness check are
deemed not effective.
6.4.6.1 QA works with the CAPA Owner to identify an alternate CAPA for CAPAs deemed
not effective or require additional investigation. The original CAPA should be
referenced by number and summary detail in the new CAPA, for cross reference
purposes.
6.4.7 QA archives all CAPA records and supporting documentation.
6.5 Target Due Date Extension
6.5.1 If the CAPA nears the target due date and the CAPA actions will not be fully completed by
that date, the CAPA Owner requests an extension from QA prior to exceeding the due
date.
6.5.2 The CAPA Owner provides a justification for the extension and a proposed new target
completion date and records the new target completion date in the Extension
Request/Justification section of FRM-007-01 CAPA Form or in the system of record.
6.5.2.1 The extension request must be approved by the Head of Quality Assurance (or
designee). QA records the new target date on FRM-007-01 CAPA Form or in the
system of record.
6.6 CAPA Tracking Log
6.6.1 QA ensures that CAPAs are tracked, either with the TMP-007-01 CAPA Tracking Log or in
the system of record, which contains, at a minimum, the following information:
• CAPA Tracking Number
• Date Initiated
• Date of Incident
• Associated Deviation Tracking Number (DTN), if applicable
• Impacted Product(s)
• Description of CAPA
• Target Due Date
• CAPA Owner (Name/Functional Area)
• Date of CAPA Approval/Closure
• Date of Effectiveness Check Closure
6.7 Tracking and Trending
6.7.1 On a periodic basis, at minimum annually, QA reviews ongoing CAPAs and ERs for any
significant trends that potentially impact Netramind compliance to current processes.
6.7.2 QA reports open, extended, overdue and approved/closed CAPAs and Effectiveness
Checks (as applicable) to Senior Management at Management Review meetings or as
agreed upon frequency.
