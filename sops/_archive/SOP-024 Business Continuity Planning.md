# SOP-024 — Business Continuity Planning

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the standard approach for maintaining critical business functions and supporting recovery
in the event of a disaster to minimize potential loss and restore Netramind’s business processes as quickly as possible.
2 SCOPE
This procedure applies to business continuity activities associated with Netramind operations supporting GxP-regulated activities, including
continuity of critical personnel, cloud-hosted systems, communications, data access, and client deliverables during significant operational
disruptions.
3 REFERENCES
• SOP-023 IT Disaster Recovery
• SOP-004 GxP Records Retention
• International Conference on Harmonization (ICH) E6 (R3) Good Clinical Practice
4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Business Continuity Plan (BCP)—A plan that defines how business operations should function to ensure that key products and
services continue to be delivered under abnormal circumstances (during a disaster or emergency).
4.2 Disaster— A significant operational, technical, environmental, or security-related event that disrupts normal business activities
and may require recovery or continuity actions.
4.3 Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) — An approved plan for recovering critical operations in the event of a major failure
(hardware/ software), destruction of facilities, and/or injured employees.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Senior Management or Designee
5.1.1 Provides oversight and support for business continuity activities and major recovery decisions.
5.1.2 Reviews and approves business continuity planning activities where appropriate.
5.2 Technical Team or Designee
5.2.1 Supports restoration of critical systems, infrastructure, and data access where applicable.
5.2.2 Coordinates recovery activities for business-critical technology resources.
5.3 Quality Assurance (QA) or Designee
5.3.1 Reviews business continuity activities and related documentation where applicable to GxP-regulated systems and
operations.
5.4 Employees and Contractors
5.4.1 Follow business continuity and emergency communication instructions during operational disruptions.
5.4.2 Report significant operational issues or disruptions to appropriate personnel.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 Appropriate personnel may coordinate business continuity and recovery activities depending on the nature and severity of the
disruption.
6.2 Relevant personnel may receive business continuity awareness or recovery-related instruction where appropriate.
6.3 Incident Reporting:
6.3.1 Significant operational disruptions may be communicated and managed through applicable incident management,
communication, and recovery processes.
6.4 In the event of a significant operational disruption, appropriate personnel assess the impact to critical business operations,
systems, data access, client deliverables, and personnel availability.
Recovery priorities and continuity activities are determined based on operational impact, business needs, data integrity
considerations, and ongoing client obligations.
6.5 Restoration of Normal Operations
6.5.1 Systems, operations, and business activities are returned to normal operational status following completion of recovery
and continuity activities where appropriate.
6.6 Testing and Preparedness
6.6.1 Business continuity activities and recovery capabilities may be periodically reviewed or discussed based on operational
risk, infrastructure complexity, business needs, and organizational changes.
6.6.2 Identified gaps or improvement opportunities may be addressed through applicable incident, CAPA, deviation, or
change control processes where appropriate.
