# SOP-003 — GxP Training Program and Curricula

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the training program for personnel performing GxP-
relevant activities, including clinical data analysis and software development at Netramind.
2 SCOPE
This SOP applies to all Netramind personnel, contractors, and consultants who require training to
perform assigned GxP tasks and duties, and describes the procedure for assigning training,
documenting training activities, and measuring training effectiveness.
This SOP does not apply to study- or project-specific training.

4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Contractor/ Consultant—An individual hired to provide a service to Netramind.
4.2 Core Training—A list of required GxP training for personnel as defined by Functional
Area (FA) Heads/ Managers.
4.3 Curriculum—A plan detailing what training is required (ex: SOPs or job skills) for each
job description in a department to ensure employees can perform their functional
responsibilities.
4.4 Electronic Quality Management System (eQMS)—A software based application that
maintains and manages a formalized system that defines and documents processes,
procedures, and responsibilities for achieving quality policies, practices, and objectives.
4.5 Job Description (JD)—A document that describes the duties, responsibilities,
contributions, outcomes, required qualifications, and reporting relationship of a particular
job.
4.6 Learning Management System (LMS)–A software application for the administration,
documentation, tracking, reporting, automation, and delivery of educational courses and
training programs.
4.7 Scope of Work (SOW)— Describes in detail the work to be performed by CSP(s).
4.8 Subject Matter Expert (SME)— individuals with highest level of expertise/competency in
skills or performing specialized tasks who can provide specific detailed knowledge or
expertise.
4.9 Trainer—An individual with appropriate education, training, and experience required to
conduct training for GxP personnel.
4.10Training—Activities conducted to gain the skills necessary to perform one’s job functions
and understanding of required GxP regulations, best practices, and Netramind’s
procedures.
4.10.1Annual Compliance Training—Training that focuses on specific topics related to
regulatory and GxP requirements and industry trends administered at least once per
calendar year.
4.10.2E-Learning—Computer-enabled transfer of knowledge and skills to the learner.
This training method does not require face-to-face interaction.
4.10.3External Training—Any non-Netramind-provided seminar, conference, or
workshop.
4.10.4Instructor-Led Training (ILT)—Training facilitated in-person either in a classroom
setting or via phone conferences or online webinars.
4.10.5Job-Specific Training—GxP training requirements specific to an individual’s job.
4.10.6On-the-Job Training—Training of GxP personnel while an instructor is performing
a hands-on demonstration.
4.11Training Curriculum—A plan detailing training that is required for each JD to ensure
employees can perform their functional responsibilities.
4.12Training File—A file for GxP personnel that includes, but is not limited to, the following:
• CV/ resume
• JD or SOW
• Training curriculum
• Training log/forms
• Professional license and/or certificate documentation, if necessary
• Documentation of external training
4.13Training Matrix—A list of controlled documents and training requirements that are
minimally required for each GxP role.
4.14Training Record—Document containing evidence that training was completed and that
GxP personnel acknowledges their understanding of the material.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Functional Area (FA) Management
5.1.1 Coordinate with Human Resources (HR) and QA to create and approve JDs and
curricula for GxP personnel.
5.1.2 Create SOWs for external contractors retained to support GxP activities.
5.1.3 Work with individual functional team members, QA, Document Management, and
HR to create, review, discuss, and update and approve GxP training plans.
5.1.4 Identify available GxP learning and development courses for employees.
5.1.5 Ensure that direct reports receive and complete required training, that periodic
training compliance reviews are performed, and that training is fulfilled prior to a
person’s independent performance of assigned GxP tasks and duties.
5.2 GxP Personnel or Designee
5.2.1 Complete mandatory training courses before the assigned due dates.
5.2.2 Complete and forwards complete Training Records for required training activities
to Document Management/QA for archiving.
5.2.3 Submit current CVs/resume and JDs to Document Management/QA for archiving.
5.2.4 Conduct assigned GxP tasks and duties according to completed training.
5.3 Document Management/QA or Designee
5.3.1 Maintain all GxP personnel training files.
5.3.2 Maintain the TMP-003-01 Training Matrix and notify GxP personnel of new or
revised controlled documents and any training deficiencies identified via oversight
activities.
5.3.3 Ensure up to date assignment of GxP/compliance training to current and new
employees and consultants.
5.4 Human Resources (HR) or Designee
5.4.1 Notify Document Management/QA of new hires, changes in roles, exiting
employees, or leaves of absence of employees who engage in GxP activities.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 General Training Program
6.1.1 Employees and consultants must be trained in specific areas of their job functions
prior to executing those related tasks and duties.
6.1.2 Training requirements for GxP personnel are determined by role.
6.1.3 Document Management/QA documents and archives all Training Records.
6.1.4 Training is conducted by a qualified person or persons identified by FA Heads, or
it can be conducted (self-administered) by employees/consultants.
6.1.5 FA Heads define and review training curricula for all staff annually or when there
are changes to approved responsibilities and roles.
6.1.6 The training program includes the following:
6.1.6.1 Training is assigned to employees and contractors according to the TMP-
003-01 Training Matrix and documented on either the FRM-003-02 Group
Training Form or the FRM-003-01 Individual Training Form.
6.1.6.2 Completed forms are forwarded to Document Management/QA. Training
may also be monitored and documented electronically via Learning
Management System (LMS) or electronic Quality Management System
(eQMS).
6.1.6.3 GCP, GDP, GMP, GLP, etc. training—Typically conducted once a year
covering new material or as refresher training via Instructor Led Training
(ILT) or e-learning modules.
6.1.6.4 SOP and WI training—GxP personnel read and acknowledge documents
applicable to their department and job.
6.1.6.5 On-the-Job Training—Provided to new employees with limited experience
of the job role to which they are assigned.
6.1.6.6 External training (ex: seminars, conferences, workshops, etc.)—Can be
assigned by a manager or identified by individual GxP personnel.
6.2 Establishing Training Requirements
6.2.1 FA Heads and Document Management/QA create the SOP Training Matrix.
6.2.2 FA Heads and Document Management/QA use the SOP Training Matrix to assign
core controlled documents training assignments to Netramind employees and
consultants.
6.2.3 FA Heads collaborate with HR to create and approve JDs and curricula for GxP
personnel and SOWs for consultants.
6.2.4 Staff that complete training through the Learning Management System (LMS) or
an electronic Quality Management System (eQMS) provide a personnel signature
form according to SOP-002 Good Documentation Practices and/or electronic
signature certificate per SOP-015 21 CFR Part 11.
6.3 Completion and Documentation of Training Requirements
6.3.1 Upon hire, personnel qualifications (e.g., education and experience) must be
documented.
6.3.2 Training records must be maintained to demonstrate completion of required
training.
6.3.3 FA Heads ensure all GxP personnel receive adequate training prior to performing
assigned job tasks and duties, and training is completed within the required period.
6.3.4 GxP personnel complete assigned training according to the training curricula
established and approved by their managers and document the training prior to
independently performing specific job tasks or duties.
6.3.5 Training must be completed within a reasonable timeframe prior to independent
performance of assigned tasks.
6.3.6 GxP personnel documents external workshops/training on FRM-003-01 Individual
Training Form. Supporting documentation, whenever possible (ex: attendance
certificates and professional license registration) should be included.
6.3.7 Periodic training may be conducted to reinforce GxP requirements.
6.4 Training Evaluation and Effectiveness
6.4.1 Training effectiveness may be assessed where appropriate.
6.5 Consultants
6.5.1 FA Heads determine what training requirements are necessary for consultants.
6.5.2 If required, consultants follow the same training procedures and requirements as
Netramind employees.
6.6 Periodic Review of Training Compliance
6.6.1 Training compliance is periodically reviewed to ensure personnel remain qualified
for assigned tasks.
6.6.2 FA Management/Heads complete the following as part of the reviews:
• Ensure direct reports are current on their assigned training
• Ensure assigned training is appropriate for each job role
• Review the training file at regular intervals to verify completeness
6.6.3 If training is conducted via an electronic system, periodic reports are generated
from the LMS/QMS for review of training compliance, as well as training metrics.
6.6.3.1 Personnel must complete required training prior to performing assigned
tasks.
6.6.3.2 Document Management/QA notifies FA Heads when training completion
noncompliance (i.e., < 100% compliance) is identified.
6.6.3.3 FA Heads ensure noncompliance is remediated.
6.7 Training File Archival
6.7.1 Document Management/QA maintains training records for GxP personnel
according to SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.
