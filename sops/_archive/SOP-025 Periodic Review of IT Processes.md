# SOP-025 — Periodic Review of IT Processes

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for periodic reviews of IT processes to
ensure that the computer systems are compliant with regulations, are fit for its intended use and
satisfies company policies and procedures.
2 SCOPE
This procedure applies to periodic reviews of computerized systems and related IT processes
supporting GxP-regulated activities where periodic review is determined to be appropriate based on
system risk, complexity, and intended use.

4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Audit Trail— Metadata records that allow the appropriate evaluation of the course of
events by capturing details on actions (manual or automated) performed relating to
information and data collection and, where applicable, to activities in computerized
systems. The audit trail should show activities, initial entry and changes to data fields or
records, by whom, when and, where applicable, why. In computerized systems, the audit
trail should be secure, computer-generated and time stamped.
4.2 Business Process— A step or set of steps that make up a process performed within the
quality system (e.g., manufacturing process, CAPA, laboratory method execution).
4.3 Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)— A systematic approach that includes actions
needed to correct (“correction”), prevent recurrence (“corrective action”), and eliminate
the cause of potential nonconforming product and other quality problems (preventive
action).
4.4 Data Integrity— Data integrity includes the degree to which data fulfils key criteria of
being attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate, complete, secure and
reliable such that data are fit for purpose.
4.5 Deviation—A documented departure from an approved procedure, process, or expected
result.
4.6 Effectiveness Check—Objective, factual evidence that the CAPA action has effectively
addressed the root cause of the reported non-conformance been reduced or removed.
4.7 GxP Records— Records associated with the development, manufacture, packaging,
testing, marketing, and distribution of the active substance (i.e., API), pharmaceutical
product (i.e., drug product), or medical device that are used for clinical and commercial
purposes. This also includes registration and regulatory records for both clinical and
commercial products.
4.8 System Administrator— A person who, based on training, experience, and education, is
granted higher level of system access to properly maintain the system. This administrator
may include a System Administrator, Application Administrator, or a Business
Administrator.
4.9 System / Process Owner— The person ultimately responsible for the business process or
processes being managed.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 IT Head or Designee
5.1.1 Provides oversight for periodic review activities where appropriate
5.2 IT Manager or Designee
5.2.1 Prepare the IT Periodic Review Schedule (PRS) for each relevant GxP computer
systems.
5.2.2 Perform the periodic review of IT processes along with Process Owner.
5.2.3 Initiate CAPA or other corrective actions where appropriate based on review
findings.
5.2.4 Update IT processes based on the review findings.
5.3 Quality Assurance (QA) or Designee
5.3.1 Coordinate review and approval of deviations, CAPAs and ECs.
5.3.2 Review the updated SOPs.
5.4 Process Owner (PO) or Designee
5.4.1 Participate in the periodic review and provide the required documents to the IT
Manager for review.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 Periodic reviews may be performed for computerized systems and IT processes based on
system risk, complexity, criticality, regulatory relevance, and operational impact.
6.2 The PRS is reviewed and approved by the Head of Information Technology (IT).
6.3 The frequency for performing the periodic reviews is dependent on the system impact,
complexity, and novelty of the system. Thus, a risk-based approach must be followed
while performing periodic reviews. Higher-risk systems or processes may require more
frequent or detailed review activities.
6.4 Relevant documentation, records, system information, incidents, changes, deviations,
CAPAs, audit findings, and security or access control information may be reviewed
where appropriate based on the scope of the review.
6.5 Appropriate personnel define the scope and focus of the review activities based on
system risk and operational considerations.
6.6 Reviews may evaluate areas such as system functionality, documentation status, user
access, security controls, system changes, incidents, deviations, training, backup
activities, and operational effectiveness where applicable.
6.7 Relevant regulatory or procedural changes may be considered where appropriate during
review activities.
6.8 Significant gaps, risks, or improvement opportunities identified during review activities
may be assessed and addressed through appropriate corrective actions where applicable.
6.9 Deviations, incidents, trends, or recurring issues may be reviewed and evaluated for
corrective action where appropriate.
6.10The process reviews for a computer system can be either performed at a predefined
period as per the Periodic Review Schedule or can be via continuous monitoring and
trending e.g., monitoring incidents, deviations, changes, etc.
6.11Audit trails, security access, and related logs may be reviewed based on system risk,
regulatory relevance, and operational considerations.
6.12Significant gaps or issues identified during periodic reviews may be addressed through
CAPA, deviation, change control, or other corrective action processes where appropriate.
6.13At the conclusion of the periodic review, IT, PO prepare a report the covers the outcome
of the review, issues identified, if any and remediation required. Review outcomes and
any identified actions may be documented where appropriate.
6.14Corrective actions may be initiated and tracked where appropriate based on review
findings.
6.15QA and IT review the CAPA report and verify effectiveness of the CAPA before closing
the issue.
6.16File documentation related to the IT periodic reviews as per the SOP-004 GxP Records
Retention.
