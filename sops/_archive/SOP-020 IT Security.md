# SOP-020 — IT Security

1 PURPOSE
This SOP defines Information Technology (IT) security practices at Netramind to protect systems, data,
and user access in support of GxP-regulated activities.
2 SCOPE
This procedure applies to Netramind-owned and managed IT systems, devices, and applications used
to support business and GxP activities.
3 REFERENCES
• N/A
4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 GxP— A general term for current Good Practice and regulations combining Good Clinical Practice
(GCP), Good Laboratory Practice (GLP), Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP), and Part 11/Annex
11 compliance.
4.2 Phishing—Phishing is designed to steal personal information (usernames, password, credit card
details, etc.) and/or often money from unsuspecting public by pretending to be a trustworthy
entity in an electronic or voice communication.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 IT or Designee
5.1.1 Establishing and enforcing Netramind IT security requirements.
5.1.2 Management of all user account related activity.
5.2 Application Business Owner or Designee
5.2.1 Approving temporary regulatory inspector access.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 IT security guidelines
6.1.1 Domain Access
6.1.1.1 Netramind access including user ID and password.
6.1.2 Screensaver
6.1.2.1 After 10 minutes of inactivity, forced screensaver lock is enforced.
6.1.2.2 Login is required to unlock the screensaver.
6.1.3 Locked account or forgotten password.
6.1.3.1 Once a user violates the login rule with five (5) consecutive invalid login
attempts, it will cause an account lockout.
6.1.4 Account password expiration notification
6.1.4.1 User will be prompted fourteen (14) days prior to password expiration to change
their domain password.
6.1.5 Server access via Remote Desktop (RDP) connection
6.1.5.1 Access requests for new RDP users must be submitted via a ticket and approved
by the appropriate business owner.
6.1.6 VPN access
6.1.6.1 VPN access is granted based on role and business need.
6.1.6.2 Connection is established by using “VPN Client” software installed on user’s
Netramind provided laptop. VPN login credentials are the same as the user’s
domain credentials.
6.1.6.3 VPN connections can only be made when the Netramind provided laptop is not
connected to the internal Local Area Network (LAN) and is only connected to the
internet.
6.1.7 Wireless connection to the internal LAN
6.1.7.1 Available from within the Netramind campus.
6.1.8 Netramind data must be stored in approved, secure storage locations managed or
authorized by IT
6.1.9 Desktop software
6.1.9.1 Only authorized, licensed software may be installed on Netramind systems.
6.1.9.2 IT should be consulted for any software requirements.
6.1.9.3 To purchase software needed for the business, users should submit a Help Desk
ticket.
6.1.10 System Clocks for Computers and Servers
6.1.10.1 System clocks are synchronized with the Domain Controller servers.
6.1.11 Password management
6.1.11.1 Never write down passwords or stick under keyboard, monitor, etc.
6.1.11.2 Never provide password to anyone, only exception would be when IT is
troubleshooting local laptop profile problem. IT forces a change of password
after the task is completed.
6.1.11.3 Use a strong password.
6.1.12 Laptop security
6.1.12.1 Laptop hard disk is encrypted for securing company data.
6.1.12.2 Laptop should be locked in a desk drawer or taken home at the end of the day.
6.1.12.3 Users must take reasonable precautions to protect company devices from theft,
loss, or unauthorized access.
6.1.12.4 No personal laptops should be connected to the Netramind internal network. A
guest network is available to visitors.
6.1.13 Home Network
6.1.13.1 All devices are recommended to be protected by a firewall.
6.1.13.2 Netramind-issued devices are to be used by the assigned employee only. The
devices should not be allowed to be used by others outside of Netramind.
6.1.13.3 When using public networks, users should connect through secure methods
(e.g., VPN) where available.
6.1.14 Lost and Stolen Devices
6.1.14.1 All missing, lost, or stolen company device or a personal device (BYOD) that
contains company confidential, proprietary, restricted, and/or personally
identifiable information must be reported to IT via a Help Desk ticket.
6.1.14.2 In the event of theft, the user is obligated to complete a police report within the
jurisdiction where the incident occurred and make report readily available to IT
for an Information Security investigation.
6.1.15 Social Engineering, Phishing e-mails, links, or phone calls
6.1.15.1 Phishing can be engineered in different forms, including fake e-mail and text
messages, forgery look-alike of exact real websites, redirected URLs and links, or
solicit phone calls.
6.1.15.2 Be very careful when visiting any links in the e-mails (personal email or company
email) or on the websites, providing any information online, or being prompted
for any download.
6.1.15.3 Do not provide any system information over the phone to 3rd parties this
includes vendors we are currently working with trying to gain access to internal
architecture.
6.1.15.4 Report any suspicious phishing activities by forwarding the phishing email to IT
via a Help Desk ticket.
6.1.15.5 IT provides periodic security awareness training.
6.1.16 Temporary system access during inspection
6.1.16.1 Temporary system access may be needed for an inspector. To request temporary
account during audit or inspection:
• Requestor asks for temporary access through a Help Desk ticket,
including name and start and termination date.
• IT creates the temporary AD account and notifies requestor and QA of
the account particulars.
• Business owner or Designee for the requested system follows the
respective system administration SOP to manage the system “Read Only”
or an appropriate account request.
• IT will ensure the account has been disabled upon receiving the
notification from the Requestor or at the predetermined termination
date.
