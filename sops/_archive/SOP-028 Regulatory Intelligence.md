# SOP-028 — Regulatory Intelligence

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for monitoring, performing
impact assessment of new and revised global regulatory changes, and implementing changes
into the relevant GxP procedures at Netramind.
2 SCOPE
This procedure applies to monitoring and assessment of regulatory, guidance, or compliance-
related information that may impact Netramind GxP-regulated activities, computerized
systems, procedures, or operational processes.
Netramind may utilize external service providers, consultants, or industry resources to support
regulatory intelligence activities where appropriate; however, Netramind remains responsible
for evaluating the impact of applicable regulatory changes on its operations and quality
system.
3 REFERENCES
• SOP-001 GxP Document Control
• International Conference on Harmonization (ICH) E6 (R3) Good Clinical Practice
4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Regulatory Intelligence—Monitoring and assessment of regulatory, guidance, standards,
or compliance-related information that may impact Netramind operations, GxP-regulated
activities, computerized systems, or quality processes.
4.2 Regulatory Intelligence Impact Assessment—Evaluation of the potential effect of new or
revised regulatory information on Netramind procedures, systems, operations, or
compliance activities.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 All Personnel
5.1.1 Communicate potentially relevant regulatory, guidance, or compliance-related
updates to appropriate personnel where applicable.
5.2 Management / Functional Area Leads
5.2.1 Review significant regulatory or compliance-related information that may impact
Netramind operations or GxP-regulated activities.
5.2.2 Coordinate assessment and implementation activities where appropriate.
5.3 Quality Assurance (QA) / Designee
5.3.1 Supports evaluation of regulatory changes and associated quality system impacts
where applicable.
5.3.2 Oversees document control activities associated with implementation of applicable
regulatory changes.
5.3.3 Maintains records associated with regulatory intelligence activities where
appropriate.
5.4 Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) / Designees
5.4.1 Assist with review, assessment, and implementation activities associated with
applicable regulatory or compliance-related changes where appropriate.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 Monitoring and Reporting Regulatory Intelligence Information
6.1.1 Regulatory or compliance-related information may be identified through sources
such as:
6.1.1.1 regulatory authority websites
6.1.1.2 industry publications
6.1.1.3 conferences or professional organizations
6.1.1.4 service providers or consultants
6.1.1.5 sponsor communications
6.1.1.6 newsletters, alerts, or external notifications
6.1.1.7 internal personnel observations or discussions
6.1.2 Potentially relevant regulatory information may be communicated to appropriate
personnel for further review and assessment where applicable.
6.2 Conducting Impact Assessment
6.2.1 Appropriate personnel review regulatory or compliance-related information to
determine whether the information may impact Netramind operations,
computerized systems, procedures, quality processes, client deliverables, or GxP-
regulated activities.
6.2.2 Impact assessments may consider:
6.2.2.1 operational impact
6.2.2.2 quality system impact
6.2.2.3 data integrity considerations
6.2.2.4 training needs
6.2.2.5 procedural updates
6.2.2.6 system or process changes
6.2.2.7 sponsor or regulatory expectations
6.2.3 Significant regulatory or compliance-related changes may result in updates to
procedures, training materials, systems, operational processes, or quality
documentation where appropriate.
6.2.4 Impact assessment activities and associated actions may be documented where
appropriate.
6.3 Implementation of Regulatory Impact Assessment
6.3.1 Appropriate personnel coordinate implementation activities associated with
applicable regulatory or compliance-related changes where appropriate.
6.3.2 Implementation activities may include:
6.3.2.1 updates to procedures or documentation
6.3.2.2 training activities
6.3.2.3 updates to computerized systems or operational workflows
6.3.2.4 communications to affected personnel or service providers
6.3.2.5 change control, CAPA, or deviation activities where appropriate
6.3.3 Updates to controlled documentation are managed in accordance with applicable
document control procedures.
6.4 Handling External Impact Assessment of Regulatory Changes
6.4.1 External service providers, consultants, or contractors may be informed of
applicable regulatory or compliance-related changes where appropriate based on
the nature of services provided.
6.4.2 Netramind remains responsible for evaluating the impact of applicable regulatory
changes on its quality system, operations, and GxP-regulated activities.
