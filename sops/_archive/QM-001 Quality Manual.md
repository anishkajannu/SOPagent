# QM-001 — Quality Manual

1. PURPOSE
This Quality Manual (QM) is developed under Netramind Innovation, Inc’s (“Netramind”) Quality
Assurance (QA) Leadership based on Netramind’s current business model for oversight of Good
Clinical Practice (GCP), Good Distribution Practice (GDP), and clinical data analysis and software
development, termed collectively as GxP in this manual.
This manual provides the foundation to ensure that Netramind consistently provides products that
meet customer needs, applicable regulatory requirements, and processes for continual
improvement. The Quality Management System (QMS) is designed on a risk-based approach that
identifies, evaluates, controls, communicates, and reports risks to appropriate internal and
external stakeholders.
The purpose of this manual is to:
• Define and describe Netramind’s QMS
• Identify what data and processes underpin data integrity and clinical trial quality
• Define roles and responsibilities involved in the QMS
• Familiarize customers, vendors/contract service providers (CSP), and regulatory authorities with
the controls that are implemented at Netramind to ensure quality
2. SCOPE
This QM applies to Netramind activities supporting GxP-regulated clinical research, computerized
systems, clinical data analysis, software development, quality management, and related
operational processes.
The QMS consists of subsystems and processes intended to support data integrity, subject safety,
regulatory compliance, quality oversight, and operational effectiveness for activities performed
by Netramind and applicable Contract Service Providers (CSPs).

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1. Netramind Employees and Contracted Service Providers (CSP)
4.1.1. Maintains necessary education, training, and experience to perform job duties.
4.1.2. Follows applicable procedures pertinent to their job function.
4.1.3. Identifies quality improvement opportunities and issues and reports these observations
to Quality Assurance (QA) personnel for evaluation.
4.2. Executive and Senior Management
4.2.1. Promotes an environment in which every employee takes ownership of quality.
4.2.2. Provides ongoing QMS oversight of all activities at [Sponsor].
4.3. GxP Quality Assurance (QA)
4.3.1. Manages and oversees GxP quality activities to ensure data integrity and the rights,

4.3.6. Provides support and oversight of corrective and preventive actions (CAPAs) related to
audit findings, Quality Issues (QIs), Quality Events (QEs), and inspections.

5. QUALITY POLICY STATEMENT
Netramind is committed to providing products that meet consumer and applicable GxP regulatory
requirements. This Quality Policy provides the guidance, structure, and programs necessary to
protect data integrity and the rights, safety, and wellbeing of study subjects and patients. By
adhering to the QMS and this Quality Policy Statement, Netramind ensures ideal outcomes for
all internal and external stakeholders.
The Quality Policy is implemented through the following Quality Objectives:
• Provide adequately qualified and trained personnel to carry out required activities
• Create robust processes and systems and implement effective controls aligned with consumer
and regulatory requirements
• Provide strong management oversight
• Maintain inspection readiness appropriate to Netramind operations and GxP-regulated activities
• Focus on continuous improvement
6. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
Netramind continuously monitors the regulatory environment to identify internal procedures that
may need to be updated (SOP-028 Regulatory Intelligence). Such changes are managed through
applicable quality system and change control processes where appropriate.
QA reviews and trends quality events, CAPAs, audit findings, and other quality metrics at defined
intervals commensurate with organizational size, risk, and regulatory activity. (SOP-007 CAPA
Management).
7. MANAGEMENT REVIEW
Through periodic review of QMS performance, Netramind Management ensures that it is fit-for-
purpose, achieves all Quality objectives, and is aligned with regulatory requirements.
8. PARTNERING WITH EXTERNAL VENDORS/CONTRACT SERVICE PROVIDERS (CSP)
Netramind’s business activities can be conducted by external resources (i.e., Contract Research
Organizations [CROs], Contract Service Providers [CSPs], etc.). Netramind QA is responsible for
oversight of these vendors/CSPs through qualifications, audits, and trend analysis to ensure
compliance with applicable GxP regulatory requirements, contracts, internal procedures (SOP-
026 CSP Selection and Qualification).
Whatever GxP activities are transferred to a vendor/CSP, it is still ultimately the responsibility of
Netramind to ensure Quality throughout those activities.

9.2. Resources
9.2.1. Netramind Senior Management understands functional needs and the capabilities of
internal resources to meet those needs. Where necessary, external vendors/CSPs are
engaged to provide expert advice and support.
9.2.2. Netramind Human Resource (HR) supports operational management in staff
recruitment, onboarding, retention, annual performance reviews, and personnel
issues. HR maintains up-to-date Curriculum Vitae (CVs) and job descriptions (JDs) for
each Netramind employee.
9.3. Quality Planning
9.3.1. The purpose of the Quality Planning subsystem is to:
9.3.1.1. Define measurable quality goals and objectives.
9.3.1.2. Define core activities subject to quality control governance and oversight.
These activities may be defined in SOPs, WIs, or other controlled documents
to ensure consistent roll-out, training, implementation, and revision.
9.3.1.3. Develop Annual Audit Schedules (AAS), Audit Plans, and Audit Strategies.
9.3.1.4. Ensure mechanisms for timely and effective communication, including
escalation procedures of safety concerns.
9.3.1.5. QMS subsystems are in place to ensure that the QMS is effective and
efficient.
9.4. QMS Maintenance
9.4.1. QA is responsible for governance, oversight and management of designing, conducting,
compliance, recording, and reporting GxP and clinical research activities.
9.4.2. Senior Management:
9.4.2.1. Participates in the design, implementation, monitoring, and maintenance of
the QMS.
9.4.2.2. Communicates the Quality Policy to all employees and for ensuring full
understanding of, and commitment to, Quality.
9.4.2.3. Ensures that all quality-related activities comply with GxP regulatory
requirements.
9.4.2.4. Participates in Management Review meetings (SOP-005 Management
Review Meetings).
9.4.2.5. Provides adequate resources to implement and maintain quality systems
and continually improve their effectiveness.
9.5. General Employee Compliance
9.5.1. Accountable for GxP compliance and ensuring consistent implementation of QMS
within their Functional Area (FA).
9.5.2. Timely escalation of potentially serious Quality Issues (QIs) according to SOP-006
Deviation Management, or SOP-029 Potential Fraud and Serious Misconduct.
9.5.3. Implementing appropriate procedures and reducing inconsistencies for continuous
improvement.
9.6. QMS Documentation
9.6.1. See Section 10 of this Manual
9.7. Infrastructure
9.7.1. Infrastructure and facilities, such as buildings, workspaces, and utilities must be
appropriate and properly maintained.
9.7.2. Maintenance and installation services are performed on premises at regular intervals.
9.7.3. Building security, business continuity, maintenance, and access control are maintained
according to SOP-024 Business Continuity Planning.
9.7.4. Information Technology (IT) provides computer, network, and online communication
support according to SOP-020 IT Security and SOP-022 IT Network Management.
9.7.5. Functional Area Managers identify the need for new or modification of existing
infrastructure within their department. Any requests are documented and submitted
to Senior Management for approval.
9.8. Information Technology (IT) and Computerized Systems Controls
9.8.1. The IT quality system defines procedures related to IT systems creation, implementation,
and maintenance. IT Control procedures are described in the following SOPs:
9.8.1.1. SOP-015 21 CFR Part 11
9.8.1.2. SOP-013 Computer System Validation
9.8.1.3. SOP-012 IT Change Control
9.8.1.4. SOP-024 Business Continuity Plan
9.8.1.5. SOP-023 IT Disaster Recovery
9.8.1.6. SOP-016 Data Integrity
9.8.2. The purpose of the IT Controls subsystem is to:
9.8.2.1. Ensure an efficient and effective computerized system environment while
complying with applicable regulations.
9.8.2.2. Ensure that controls are in place to prevent unauthorized access or changes
to data.
9.8.2.3. Ensure that a Business Continuity Plan is in place and that it includes all
required elements to ensure a quick recovery in case of unforeseen events.
9.8.2.4. Computerized systems used in GxP-regulated activities are validated
commensurate with their intended use and associated risk.
9.8.2.5. Ensure that changes are made according to SOPs and have authorization,
documentation, and testing prior to use.
9.8.3. Netramind applies risk-based validation principles consistent with GAMP 5 guidance to
ensure appropriate evaluation, validation, implementation, and change control of
computerized systems used in GxP-regulated activities. The level of validation is
determined based on system function, risk to data integrity and patient safety, and
regulatory impact. Where applicable, systems are assessed for compliance with 21 CFR
Part 11 requirements
9.9. Audits and Inspections
9.9.1. Audit and Inspection procedures are defined in SOP-009 Internal Audit, and SOP-028
Regulatory Agency Inspections.
9.9.2. The Audits and Inspections subsystem:
9.9.2.1. Maintains an internal system of periodic audits/ assessments at defined
intervals based on risk, organizational maturity, and regulatory activity.
9.9.2.2. Maintains a risk-based approach to the qualification and oversight of
Contract Service Providers (CSPs), including initial qualification, contractual
controls, and ongoing performance monitoring commensurate with the risk
and regulatory impact of the services provided.
9.9.2.3. Performance of QMS procedures is systematically monitored and measured
to ensure effectiveness and identify opportunities for improvement.
9.9.2.4. Maintains a system to manage Regulatory Health Authority Inspection
activities according to SOP-028 Regulatory Agency Inspections.
9.10.Document Control and Maintenance
9.10.1.The Document Control system, defined in SOP-001 GxP Document Control, ensures
that:
9.10.1.1. Documents are reviewed for adequacy and approved prior to release.
9.10.1.2. Documents are reviewed and updated at appropriate timepoints.
9.10.1.3. Version control is maintained.
9.10.1.4. Documents are distributed to and are available at appropriate locations.
9.10.1.5. Obsolete documents are withdrawn from points of use, and/or clearly
identified to prevent unintended use.
9.10.1.6. Documents remain legible and retrievable.
9.10.1.7. Document distribution is controlled.
9.10.1.8. Document archival, including appropriate retention periods and destruction
requirements, is appropriately maintained. All GxP records are stored in a
manner that provides adequate security and protection from damage
according to SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.
9.10.2.Records are established and maintained to provide evidence of conformity to
requirements and effective operation of the QMS. The Document Control system
ensures:
9.10.2.1. Records remain legible, readily identifiable, and easily retrievable
9.10.2.2. Records are organized into four categories as defined in Section 10
9.11.Risk Management
9.11.1.Risk Management is defined in SOP-030 Risk Management.
9.11.2.The purpose of the Risk Management subsystem is to:
9.11.2.1. Evaluate risks to Quality based on scientific knowledge and the potential
impact on subject/patient safety and/or data integrity.
9.11.2.2. Determine the level of effort, formality, and documentation of emerging
risks in a way that is commensurate with the level of risk.
9.11.2.3. Conducting Risk Analysis by identifying and analyzing hazards and evaluating
the impact of those hazards.
9.11.2.4. Control risk by implementing measures to reduce it to an acceptable level,
accept the risk, or mitigate its impact.
9.11.2.5. Maintain a system for reviewing risk to detect unforeseen failures.
9.11.3.QA and Netramind FAs review new and known risks, risk mitigation procedures, and the
impact of regulatory changes. FAs utilize outcomes of these reviews to make decisions,
identify priorities, and take appropriate actions to more effectively achieve Quality
objectives.
9.12.Control of Nonconforming Product
9.12.1.Product, at any stage, that does not conform to requirements and specifications is
identified and controlled to prevent its unintended use. The controls and related
responsibilities for handling nonconforming situations are defined in SOP-006
Deviation Management.
9.13.QI, QE, and CAPA Management
9.13.1.QI, QE, and CAPA Management Procedures are defined in SOP-007 CAPA Management.
9.13.2.The purpose of the QI, QE, and CAPA Management subsystem is to:
9.13.2.1. Identify, mitigate, document, resolve, and report issues that impact trial
participant safety and data integrity.
9.13.2.2. Provide a standard process to determine and eliminate the root cause of
detected and potential deviations or other undesirable situations.
9.13.2.3. Establish and maintain a system for documenting, tracking, and trending
CAPAs.
9.13.2.4. Ensure that CAPAs are evaluated for effectiveness.
9.14.Deviations and Investigations
9.14.1.Deviation and Investigation on procedures are defined in SOP-006 Deviation
Management.
9.14.2.The purpose of the Deviations and Investigations subsystem is to:
9.14.2.1. Establish and maintain a system to investigate and manage deviations from
procedures and requirements and to restrict non-conforming product.
9.14.2.2. Ensure deviations are assessed for impact on trial participant safety, public
health, and regulatory compliance.
9.14.2.3. Investigate the root cause of noncompliance that poses a significant risk to
the rights, safety, and wellbeing of trial participant and/or data integrity.
9.14.2.4. Identify actions needed to correct and prevent recurrence of
noncompliance.
9.14.2.5. Track, trend, and report deviations and investigations.
9.15.Training
9.15.1.Netramind ensures that personnel are appropriately trained and qualified to execute
their roles.
9.15.2.Training procedures are described in SOP-003 GxP Training Program and Curricula to
ensure that:
9.15.2.1. Training is conducted by qualified individuals and with sufficient frequency
so employees remain familiar with ongoing and updated requirements.
9.15.2.2. Employees perform their duties with consistency.
9.15.2.3. A system is established and maintained to document and track employee
training.
9.15.2.4. The qualification of external consultants is documented.
9.16.Contract Service Provide (CSP) Management
9.16.1.CSP Management procedures are defined in SOP-026 CSP Selection and Qualification.
9.16.2.Qualifying CSPs
9.16.2.1. Netramind qualifies CSPs based on a risk assessment that considers the type
of services provided and the impact of those services on trial participant
safety and data integrity.
9.16.3.When either the CSP’s qualification status is renewed or amended, QA documents the
updated status and communicates this to the CSP and internal departments.
9.16.4.Netramind maintains an ongoing collaboration with CSP and ensures optimized
performance through oversight and monitoring. QA, FAs, and Subject Matter Experts
(SMEs) continuously monitor service agreements, key performance indicators (KPIs),
and other compliance parameters.
9.17.Management Review
9.17.1.Netramind Management ensures that the QMS is fit for purpose, fulfills quality
objectives, remains aligned with Netramind’s business strategy, remains compliant
with GxP regulations, and is supported by appropriate resources.
9.18.Quality Improvement Initiatives
9.18.1.The purpose of this Quality Improvement Initiatives subsystem is to:
9.18.1.1. Execute and document quality improvement initiatives triggered by changes
in GxP regulatory requirements, deployment of new organizational systems,
the outcome of a quality review, audit, or Regulatory Health Authority
inspection.
9.19.Key GxP Quality Guidelines
9.19.1.Good Documentation Practice
9.19.1.1. Defined in SOP-002 Good Documentation Practice.
9.19.1.2. Netramind employees and vendors/CSPs are responsible for proper
documentation and record maintenance in accordance with applicable laws,
rules, regulations, contractual agreements, and industry standards.
9.19.2.Good Clinical Practice (GCP)
9.19.2.1. Netramind ensures that the clinical trials are planned, conducted, and
reported according to ICH E6 GCP requirements.
10. [NETRAMIND QMS DOCUMENTATION
10.1.QMS Document Hierarchy
10.1.1.The Netramind QMS is supported by documentation. The hierarchy of documents used
to support the QMS is demonstrated in Figure 1.
Figure 1: QMS Document Hierarchy
10.1.2.Level 1—The Quality Manual (QM) is the highest level document at Netramind. The
Corporate Quality Policy is stated in Section 5 of this manual.
10.1.3.Level 2—Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are departmental documents that
describe performance requirements of Netramind activities. SOPs ensure that
activities are performed in a compliant and consistent manner. Every Netramind
department has well-defined SOPs. providing instructions on:
− What needs to be done,
− The timing of when the activity needs to be performed,
− Where the activity is to be performed,
− The reason why it needs to be performed, and
− By whom.
10.1.4.Level 3—Work Instructions (WIs) are activity-specific procedures that provide more in-
depth instructions about how an activity described in an SOP is performed. Master
Batch Records provide information on the product specifications, production process
instructions and specifications, acceptance criteria, packaging and labeling
specifications.
10.1.5.Level 4—Records include files, forms, templates, etc. that capture information while
completing an activity directed by an SOP and/or a WI
− “Files” is a general term for documentation required to be retained according to
SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.
− Forms are fillable documents that have fixed text fields with defined sections.
− Templates are more flexible than Forms because they can be modified to
accommodate project-specific requirements.
10.2.Netramind Document Management oversees the Controlled Document Program development,
review, and approval process.
10.3.Functional Area (FA) groups and Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) are responsible for developing
and revising Controlled Documents within their area of expertise.
10.3.1.QA reviews SOPs and WIs and provides input based on regulatory requirements and
good industry practice during the routine document review process.
10.4.Once Controlled Documents are approved, they are maintained according to SOP-001 GxP
Document Control, SOP-004 GxP Records Retention and other internal procedures.
10.4.1.SOPs and WIs are available to staff and contractors for training purposes and Regulatory
Health Authority inspections.
10.4.2.When a new Controlled Document is released or made obsolete, Document
Management notifies all departments affected by the change. QA and the applicable
Functional Area Managers are responsible for providing training on new and/or
updated procedures.
10.5.Netramind utilizes risk-based quality principles to support quality system effectiveness,
software development, clinical research activities, and operational controls.
