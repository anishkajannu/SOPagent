# SOP-005 — Management Review Meetings

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the Quality System Management (QMS) review
process at Netramind.
2 SCOPE
This SOP applies to all GxP personnel involved in implementing, monitoring, and updating
Netramind’s QMS.

4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Management Review—A period set aside to evaluate quantifiable metrics that Netramind uses
to measure performance against strategic and operational goals.
4.2 Quality Event (QE)—A Quality Issue (QI) that has been classified as noncompliance, a deviation, a
violation, or an undesirable outcome. Each QE is triaged to determine its severity.
4.3 Quality Issue (QI)—An issue or occurrence identified from an audit, inspection, and/or within a
clinical program that has not yet been classified as a non-compliance, deviation, violation, or
undesirable outcome.
4.4 Quality Management System (QMS)— A formalized system that defines and documents
processes, procedures, and responsibilities for achieving quality policies, practices and
objectives.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Senior Management
5.1.1 Participate in Management Review Meetings (MRMs).
5.1.2 Appoint a Management Representative (MR) to facilitate MRMs.
5.2 Functional Area (FA) Management/ Department Heads/ Designee
5.2.1 Collate, analyze, and summarize quality data and presents it during MRMs.
5.2.2 Identify action items and provide resources to ensure their timely completion.
5.3 Quality Assurance (QA)
5.3.1 Presents data and summaries of Netramind internal, Contract Service Provider (CSP),
investigator site, etc. audits during MRMs.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 MRM Preparation
6.1.1 Senior Management appoints a Management Representative (MR) to facilitate MRMs.
6.1.2 Management Review Meetings are conducted at defined intervals based on
organizational needs and regulatory activity, and at least annually.
6.1.3 The MR uses the TMP 005-01 Management Review Meeting (MRM) Agenda Template to
determine and document the discussion topics and presenters. Agenda and materials are
circulated in advance of the meeting where practical.
6.2 MRM Conduct
6.2.1 Appropriate personnel, including senior management and relevant functional
representatives, participate in Management Review Meetings.
6.2.2 MRM discussions can include, but are not limited to:
• Feedback from Netramind employees on QMS functions
• Quality Objectives and Quality Policy for effectiveness
• Review of findings from internal audits and regulatory inspections
• Review of quality events and observations from operational activities
• Evaluation and/or re-evaluation of sub-contractors or CSPs
• Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) and Effectiveness Check (EC) systems
• Areas of improvement and future development of the QMS
• Review of the Quality Manual, if necessary
• QMS resource issues
• Review of feedback and satisfaction surveys, if any
6.2.3 Recommendations, discussions, action items, completion dates, and responsible
personnel are documented on the MRM Agenda and verified prior to adjourning.
6.3 MRM Follow-up Activities
6.3.1 Meeting records are maintained as controlled documents.
6.3.2 Meeting minutes, presentations, meeting records, and other associated documents are
stored in a secure location by QA according to SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.
