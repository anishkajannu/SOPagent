1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This guide explains how clinical sites use the NORC Site Portal to complete site certification, register subjects,
submit ophthalmic imaging data, respond to Reading Center queries, and—when the study allows it—transfer
bulk data packages.
1.2 Intended Audience
Site investigators, coordinators, photographers, and other clinical site staff who have been assigned to a study
or assist with imaging upload/query resolution in the NORC Site Portal.
1.3 Site and Reading Center Responsibilities
Clinical Site Users Reading Center (NORC)
Create an account and sign in Approves your account
Complete site certification (when required) Reviews and certifies the site
Acknowledge study documents (when required) Publishes documents and contacts
Register subjects and submit imaging Performs technical QC on submissions
Respond to queries Opens and closes queries
Submit data dumps (when enabled) Receives and processes transfers
1.4 Prerequisites
Before you begin, make sure you have:
 A workstation with a supported modern web browser and a reliable internet connection
 Access to the email inbox you will use for portal verification codes and study notifications
 The study imaging requirements (Imaging Acquisition Manual and related documents), as provided for each
study
2. Getting Started
2.1 Create a Site User Account (Sign Up)
Purpose: Request access to the NORC Site Portal. New accounts will remain pending until an administrator from
the Reading Center approves them.
Steps:
1. Open the portal norc.netramind.ai. (Figure 1)
2. Select Create account. (Figure 2)
3. Enter your email address and request a verification code.
4. Enter the one-time code from your email.
5. Complete your profile: name, password, security questions, and role.
6. For a clinical site account, enter your site name and location when prompted.
7. Submit the registration. You will see a message confirming that your account is awaiting approval.
Figure 1. NORC portal landing page
Figure 2. Create account page
Notes:
 Choose the clinical site option (not sponsor or grader) when signing up as site staff. This will prevent any
delays in reviewing your access request.
 You cannot sign in until the Reading Center sets your account to active. You should receive an email when
your access is reviewed by an administrator.
 If approval is delayed, contact your Reading Center project contact (see Appendix C).
2.2 Sign In (Login + MFA)
Purpose: Securely access your account using your email and password, followed by a multi-factor authentication
process involving a one-time verification code sent to your registered email.
Steps:
1. Open the portal login page. (Figure 3)
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. Enter the verification code from your email.
4. After successful multi-factor authentication (MFA), you land on Assigned Studies.
Figure 3. Login/sign-up MFA page
Notes:
 Ensure you have access to the registered email address linked to your account, as verification codes are sent
to this address and remain valid for 10 minutes.
 If login fails after your account has been approved, confirm that you are using the approved email address
and contact support if the problem persists.
2.3 Reset Password
Purpose: Regain access to your account if you forget your password. Password reset is available for active
accounts only.
Steps:
1. On the login page, select Forgot password.
2. Enter your email and request a verification code.(Figure 5)
3. Enter the code from your email. (Figure 4)
4. Answer your security questions.
5. Set a new password and confirm it.
6. Sign in with the new password (including MFA).
Figure 4. One-Time code email
Figure 5. Reset password
2.4 Sign out
Open the user menu in the header (your name or avatar area) and select Sign Out.
2.5 Session timeout (Idle Warning and Auto Logout)
After a period of inactivity, the portal displays a Still there? warning with a countdown. Select Stay signed in to
continue your session. If no action is taken before the countdown expires, you will be signed out automatically
and returned to the login page.
Notes:
 It is generally recommended to finish uploads or other multi-step work before stepping away for an
extended period.

3. If acknowledgment is required, confirm acknowledgment (including name and password when

 When a new study document is published that requires acknowledgment, your site may receive an in-app
notification.

7. Automatic Slot Check
Purpose: Slot Check confirms that the file you attached matches the imaging slot requirements—for example
the correct eye (laterality), modality, resolution, and that the file contains only one scan. It runs as soon as you
select a file on an imaging slot where Slot Check is enabled. It does not replace Automatic Grade Check,
technical QC, queries, or grader review.
Slot Check is as per study design. When it is enabled for a slot, use the statuses and messages on the imaging
upload page to correct mismatches while the patient is still available when possible.
When both Slot Check and Grade Check are enabled for the same slot, Slot Check runs first. Grade Check starts
only after Slot Check shows Passed.

7.3 Submitting while Slot Check is still running or has failed
You are not blocked from Review & Submit.
• If any attached slot is still Processing (Slot Check and/or Grade Check), you will see a confirmation that
Automatic Slot Check or Grade Check is still in process. You may proceed. Prefer waiting briefly on the
imaging page for Slot Check to finish when you can.
• If any attached slot is Failed or Failed to process, you will see: One or more imaging slots failed
automatic slot or grade check. Do you want to proceed with submission? Prefer Re-upload after
correcting the file. Proceed only if you must submit now (for example, the visit window is closing).
If both apply, one confirmation covers both messages. Choose Stay on this page to re-upload, or Proceed to
continue to review, attestation, and password confirmation as usual.
Notes:
• Slot Check is enabled only for slots configured that way for your study (as per study design). If a slot has
no Slot Check label, this feature does not apply to that slot.
• Slot Check runs per imaging slot, not on the whole visit at once.
• When Slot Check and Grade Check are both enabled, fix Slot Check failures first; Grade Check will not
start until Slot Check Passed.
• Confirm eye, modality, and resolution against the Imaging Acquisition Manual before uploading to
reduce Slot Check failures.

review Grade Check Results while the subject is still available so that failed images can be reacquired. (Figure 27)

8.2 Submitting while Grade Check is still running or has failed
 Automatic Grade Check does not prevent a site from proceeding to Review & Submit.
 If one or more files are still Processing, the portal displays a confirmation message indicating that Automatic
Grade Check is still in progress. You may proceed with the submission and review the final results later
under Grade Check.
 If one or more files show Failed or Failed to Process, the portal displays a confirmation message before
allowing you to proceed. When possible, select Stay on this page and use Re-upload to replace the affected
file.
 If submission cannot be delayed (for example, because the visit window is closing), select Proceed to
continue to review, attestation, and submission.
 If both conditions apply, the portal displays a single confirmation message addressing the outstanding Grade
Check results.
8.3 Grade Check tab (left menu)
Purpose: See Automatic Grade Check results for your site across subjects, visits, and imaging slots, including files
you already submitted while a check was still running.
Steps:
1. Open Grade Check in the left sidebar. (Figure 28)
2. Use the status checkboxes to show Passed, Failed, Processing, and/or Failed to process.
3. Use Refresh in the header when you want the latest statuses.
4. For Failed or Failed to process, select Re-upload to open imaging upload for that subject, visit, and slot.
5. For other statuses, select View upload to open the applicable imaging submission.

 Grade Check runs per imaging slot, not on the whole visit at once. One slot can pass while another is still
processing or has failed.
 Automatic Grade Check is immediate quality control for the site. Reading Center technical QC and graded
reads still occur after the submission is received.

9.2 Respond to Queries
Steps:
1. Open the query and read the Reading Center request (type and comments). (Figure 30)
2. Choose a response category: Provide written response, Upload replacement image, Confirm image cannot

3. Enter your written response.
4. If you are uploading replacements, attach files to the indicated slots.
5. Enter your name, date, and password, then submit.
Figure 30. Open Query page showing query details and options to submit response

The query status changes to Awaiting Reading Center while your response is reviewed. Once resolved, the
query moves to Closed. If additional information is needed, the Reading Center may reopen the query.
Notes:
 Select the response category that best matches the Reading Center’s request.
 Retain confirmation numbers and query IDs in accordance with your site SOPs.
