# SOP-006 — Deviation Management

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the procedure for reporting, documenting,
reviewing, approving, tracking, and trending of GxP deviations at Netramind.
2 SCOPE
This SOP applies to deviations reported from Netramind internal policies and procedures and, where
applicable, deviations identified through activities performed by or involving Contract Service
Providers.
3 REFERENCES
• SOP-007 CAPA Management
• SOP-004 GxP Records Retention
• ICH GCP E6(R3)
4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Contract Service Provider (CSP) — A person or organization (commercial, academic or other)
providing a service used during the conduct of a clinical trial to either the sponsor or the
investigator to fulfil one or more of their related activities.
4.2 Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)— A systematic approach that includes actions needed
to correct (“correction”), prevent recurrence (“corrective action”), and eliminate the cause of
potential nonconforming product and other quality problems (preventive action).
4.3 Date of Discovery—The date on which a deviation was discovered. Target due dates of resolution
activities are calculated based on the date of discovery.
4.4 Date of Occurrence—The date on which a deviation first occurred.
4.5 Deviation—Any departure from an approved procedure, process, or expected result. Deviations
may be:
4.5.1 Planned—A pre-planned or expected departure from procedures that require an approval
from Functional Area (FA) Management and Quality Assurance (QA)
4.5.2 Unplanned—Deviations detected by project teams through direct oversight activities and
through audit or inspection activities.
4.6 Immediate Actions—Actions taken to mitigate a deviation and bring a system/ process back into
conformance.
4.7 Root Cause— The core issue that caused noncompliance, a deviation, violation, or undesirable
outcome.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Initiator
5.1.1 Notify Quality Assurance (QA) of the deviation.
5.1.2 Initiate a Deviation Report by completing Section A of the FM-006-01 Deviation Report
Form or in the system of record, as applicable.
5.1.3 Coordinate with QA to determine if a CAPA is required and identifies appropriate actions.
5.1.4 Circulate the Deviation Report Form for approval, as applicable.
Note: The Initiator is not necessarily the person who identified the deviation.
5.2 Functional Area (FA) Management
5.2.1 Ensure that deviations are accurately documented prior to approving the Deviation
Report Form.
5.2.2 Coordinate with the Initiator to complete the Impact Assessment and Impact Justification
sections in the Deviation Report.
5.2.3 Review the Deviation Report for completeness and approves the form or electronically in
the system of record to proceed further through the process.
5.3 Quality Assurance (QA)
5.3.1 QA reviews deviations to ensure appropriate documentation, assessment, and follow-up
actions are in place.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 Deviation Management
6.1.1 Appropriate change controls are implemented if the planned change is a permanent
change to a document or process.
6.1.2 The Initiator completes the FRM-006-01 Deviation Report Form as soon as reasonably
possible after discovery.
6.1.3 The Initiator completes Section A of the Deviation Report Form or system of record,
including:
6.1.3.1 Date of deviation. FA Management and QA approve the deviation before
execution if the deviation is planned. The Date(s) of Deviation is the date on
which the deviation is understood to have occurred or to occur (if planned).
6.1.3.2 Deviation details, including the who, what, when, where, and why, as applicable,
all impacted activities.
6.1.3.3 A list of all immediate actions taken in response to the deviation, if any.
6.1.3.4 A summary and dates of the investigation.
6.1.3.5 A reasonable assessment of the likely cause should be documented.
6.1.4 Planned deviations must be reviewed and approved prior to execution, with
consideration of potential impact.
6.1.5 The Initiator indicates whether CAPA is required, where appropriate.
6.1.5.1 Follows SOP-007 CAPA Management.
6.1.6 The Initiator reviews the Deviation Report for completeness and accuracy.
6.1.7 The Initiator forwards the Deviation Report to QA for initial review to obtain a Deviation
Tracking Number (DTN), as applicable. The system of record may automatically assign
deviation tracking numbers.
6.1.8 If required, QA provides a unique, sequential DTN in the following format: DEV-YY-XXX
• YY - denotes the last two (2) digits of the current year.
• XXX - denotes a three-digit sequential tracking number beginning with 001. This
number resets each year to begin with 001.
• Ex: DEV-25-001 is the DTN of the first deviation of 2025.
6.1.9 The Initiator signs and dates the Deviation Report Form or system of record and
submits/forwards the form or system of record to the appropriate FA Management for
review.
6.1.10 FA Management reviews the Deviation Report.
6.1.10.1 FA Management coordinates with the Initiator to complete impact assessment.
6.1.10.2 If the Deviation Report requires revisions, Functional Area Management returns
it to the Initiator.
6.1.11 When the Deviation Report is accurate and complete, FA Management signs, dates, and
forwards/submits it to QA.
6.1.12 QA reviews Section A of the Deviation Report Form or system of record. If it is acceptable,
QA records the assigned DTN and applicable information in the TMP-006-01 Deviation
Log.
6.1.13 QA completes Section B of the Deviation Report Form or system of record.
6.1.14 QA reviews the deviation documentation and ensures that the investigation is complete
and accurate:
6.1.14.1 Ensures conclusions for the root cause are technically sound, scientifically
justified, and meet compliance expectations.
6.1.14.2 Verifies that all referenced documentation supports conclusions.
6.1.14.3 Reviews CAPA Plans and ensures the recommended CAPA is specific,
measurable, appropriate, timely, and has responsible owners.
6.1.14.4 Approve the deviation if all documentation is acceptable, the investigation is
complete, and conclusions are accurate.
6.1.14.5 All deviation investigations are targeted to be completed in a timely manner.
Deviation investigation extensions may be documented where justified.
6.2 Records
6.2.1 Deviation files will be maintained by QA according to SOP-004 GxP Records Retention.
