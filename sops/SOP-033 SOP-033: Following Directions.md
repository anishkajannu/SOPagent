# SOP-033 — SOP-033: Following Directions

## 1. Purpose
To establish a standardized approach for Netramind clinical site staff and reading center personnel to correctly follow written instructions, manuals, and protocol-specific directions when performing imaging acquisition, file export, and portal-based tasks, thereby ensuring consistency, data integrity, and compliance with study-specific requirements.

## 2. Scope
This SOP applies to all Netramind clinical site staff who acquire, export, or submit ophthalmic imaging data using instructions provided in device manuals (e.g., MAN-018-02) and to all users of the NORC Site Portal (MAN-018-01) who must follow on-screen and written directions to complete study-related tasks. This SOP does not replace the study protocol or device-specific manuals; it governs how directions within those documents are to be followed and escalated when unclear.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Clinical Site Imaging Technician: Follows the step-by-step scan parameter checks and acquisition sequence exactly as written in MAN-018-02, including confirming Field of View, Mode, and Resolution settings before pressing ACQUIRE.
- Clinical Site Coordinator: Confirms that staff refer to the study-specific Imaging Acquisition Manual and Documents, per MAN-018-01, before consulting general portal FAQ text.
- Portal User: Follows the NORC Site Portal Help tab instructions in sequence (open Help, read guidance, enter message) when directions in the manual are unclear, per MAN-018-01.
- Netramind Reading Center Support Staff: Reviews Contact Support messages submitted through the portal and responds to the email address associated with the user's account.
- Quality Assurance Reviewer: Verifies that acquired images and exported files reflect adherence to the documented step order and parameter checks specified in MAN-018-02.

## 4. Procedure
1. Identify the applicable source document for the task at hand (e.g., MAN-018-02 for SD-OCT image acquisition and file export, or MAN-018-01 for NORC Site Portal tasks).
2. Read the full relevant section of the source document before beginning the task, rather than acting on a single step in isolation.
3. Confirm that portal features or protocol-specific imaging requirements are enabled or applicable for the current study by checking the study protocol and applicable study materials, as directed in MAN-018-01.
4. Follow each instructional step in the exact order presented in the source document (e.g., verify Eye selection, Field of View at 30°, Mode as OCT Volume, and Resolution as High Speed before pressing ACQUIRE, per MAN-018-02).
5. Complete one action per step before proceeding to the next step; do not combine or skip steps listed in the manual.
6. If both eyes require scanning, repeat the applicable acquisition steps for the opposite eye only after confirming the first scan is complete, per MAN-018-02.
7. When a directional step or on-screen prompt is ambiguous, consult the study-specific Imaging Acquisition Manual and Documents first, rather than relying solely on general FAQ content, per MAN-018-01.
8. If ambiguity remains after consulting the study-specific manual, open the Help tab in the NORC Site Portal and access the Contact Support section.
9. Enter a clear description of the question or issue in the Message text area of the Contact Support form.
10. Await a reply to the email address associated with the portal account; if portal access is unavailable, use the offline contacts listed in Appendix C of MAN-018-01.
11. Document any deviation from the written directions, including the reason and resolution, in the site's imaging or portal activity log for quality review.
