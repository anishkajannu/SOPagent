# SOP-026 — Contract Service Provider Selection and Qualification

1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the selection, evaluation, and qualification of GxP
contract service providers (CSP), also refers to clinical research organizations (CROs), that provide
material and/or services to Netramind.
This SOP also describes the management of CSP contracts and maintenance of the Approved Service
Provider (ASP) List.
2 SCOPE
This procedure applies to the selection, evaluation, qualification, and management of external service
providers supporting Netramind GxP-regulated activities, including technology providers, consultants,
cloud service providers, and outsourced operational services where applicable.

4 DEFINITIONS
4.1 Approved Service Providers List (ASP)—Current active and approved CSPs and suppliers that
provide a specific material or GxP service(s) to Netramind.
4.2 Contract Service Provider (CSP)— A person or organization (commercial, academic or other)
providing a service used during the conduct of a clinical trial to either the sponsor or the
investigator to fulfil one or more of their related activities. Examples of a CSP include:
4.2.1 Clinical Research Organization (CRO) — A person or an organization (commercial,
academic, or other) contracted by the sponsor to perform one or more of a sponsor's
trial-related duties and functions.
4.2.2 Service Provider (SP)—A person or organization (commercial, academic, or other)
providing a service used either by the sponsor or the investigator to fulfill trial-related
activities.
4.3 Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) / Confidential Disclosure Agreement (CDA)—A legal contract
that outlines confidential material, intellectual, or proprietary knowledge that the parties can
share but are restricted from generalized and external use.
4.4 Quality Agreement— A contract which states the manufacturing, quality control, regulatory and
any GxP provisions required to provide materials, products, equipment, study related products
or services in adherence with Netramind. and regulatory requirements. The obligations and
responsibilities should be stated as part of this agreement. Also referred to as Quality Technical
Agreement (QTA), Quality Assurance Agreement (QAA), or Clinical Quality Agreement (CQA).
4.5 Request for Proposal (RFP)—An outline of required services and expectations used to request
service provider bids for products, solutions, and services.
4.6 Master Services Agreement (MSA)—A legally binding service-oriented contract between two or
more parties that states the responsibilities and obligations of one party to another. The MSA
contains terms and conditions that will be applicable and incorporated by reference into future
work orders or supplemental agreements.
4.7 Scope of Work (SOW)—Describes in detail the work to be performed by CSP(s) for Netramind.
5 RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Netramind Personnel
5.1.1 Use approved service providers where qualification or evaluation is required.
5.2 CSP Selection Team
5.2.1 Identify and evaluate potential service providers.
5.2.2 Coordinate requests for proposals, service agreements, and scopes of work where
applicable.
5.2.3 Assess operational and technical suitability of service providers.
5.3 Quality Assurance (QA)
5.3.1 Support risk-based evaluation and qualification activities where applicable.
5.3.2 Maintain the Approved Service Provider (ASP) List.
5.3.3 Review quality or compliance-related concerns associated with service providers.
5.4 Legal or Management Designee
5.4.1 Supports review and execution of applicable service agreements and contracts where
appropriate.
6 PROCEDURE
6.1 CSP Identification and Due Diligence
6.1.1 Functional Area Management or CSP Selection Team complete the Request for Proposal
(RFP) and send it to CSP(s) and ensure an NDA or CDA is in place prior to discussing any
proprietary information with a CSP.
6.1.2 Functional Area Management or CSP Selection Team may also send Questionnaires to the
CSP(s) undergoing evaluation for specific inquiries on services.
6.1.3 The CSP Selection Team identify potential CSPs by comparing a CSP’s capabilities and
competencies through the evaluation of the following information:
6.1.3.1 Request for proposal (RFP), where applicable
6.1.3.2 Completed CSP questionnaire
6.1.3.3 Technical capabilities
6.1.3.4 Operational performance/reputation/time in business
6.1.3.5 Business capabilities
6.1.3.6 Financial stability
6.1.3.7 Available company information, e.g., regulatory inspection history, quality and
compliance history, and licensing.
6.2 Initiating CSP Selection
6.2.1 Qualification activities may be initiated when onboarding a new service provider or
expanding the scope of services provided by an existing provider.
6.2.2 If the CSP is already approved by one Netramind functional area, documentation of the
prior qualification assessment may be used, if appropriate.
6.3 CSP Evaluation Requirements
6.3.1 Service providers may be evaluated using a risk-based approach considering factors such
as:
• nature of services provided
• impact on GxP activities or data integrity
• technical capabilities
• security and confidentiality considerations
• operational reliability
• prior experience or compliance history
Evaluations may include questionnaires, meetings, documentation review, security
assessments, or other qualification activities where appropriate.
6.4 Managing CSP Contracts
6.4.1 Functional Area Management coordinates with Legal to draft a Master Service Agreement
(MSA), or Statement of Work (SOW), or other applicable contractual document, prior to
starting any activities at the CSP.
6.4.2 The contract documents key study expectations (i.e., study/ project objectives, timelines,
key milestones, requirements for deliverables, data protection, etc.).
6.4.3 Functional Area Management distributes the SOW to the selected CSP for review.
Relevant project documentation (e.g., draft or final study protocol) may be shared with
the CSP.
6.4.4 Functional Area Management determines if changes are needed to study contracts and
notifies QA and RA of potential contractual changes.
6.4.5 Legal is responsible for management of service agreements and contracts for execution in
addition to maintaining and filing all contracts in the designated Legal repository
6.5 Quality Agreement Requirements for GCP, GVP CSPs
6.5.1 Quality or technical agreements may be established with service providers where
appropriate based on the nature of services provided and associated regulatory or
operational risks.
6.6 Approved Service Provider (ASP) List
6.6.1 QA maintains an approved and version-controlled ASP List TMP-026-01 Approved Service
Provider List.
6.6.2 QA updates the ASP List as CSPs are added and/or when a CSP status is changed.
6.6.3 QA conducts periodic review of the ASP List to ensure the following information is
accurate:
6.6.3.1 CSP name and address
6.6.3.2 Material or services provided
6.6.3.3 Qualification status
6.6.3.4 Current Status (active/inactive)
6.6.3.5 Last compliance assessment or audit date
6.6.4 Re-evaluation of a CSP may be performed sooner as a result of critical observations made
from an on-site audit, observations issued from a regulatory inspection, or trends
witnessed from other Netramind systems.
6.6.5 Restrictions, concerns, or required follow-up actions associated with conditionally
approved providers may be documented and managed appropriately.
6.7 Changes within a CSP
6.7.1 Notification of CSP organization changes in ownership or name change that have no
impact on service provided will be filed within QA. No evaluation/re-evaluation is
necessary.
6.7.2 Organizational changes that result in an impact on service will require CSP evaluation
according to Section 6.2.
6.7.3 Any other administrative changes, including mailing address or CSP contacts, can be
updated on the next evaluation/re-evaluation form. No evaluation/re-evaluation is
necessary.
6.8 Records
6.8.1 Legal maintains copies of all contracts in a secure folder.
6.8.2 QA maintains all versions of the Approved Service Providers (ASP) List
6.8.3 Functional Area Leads ensure that CSP communication (ex: CSP presentations, bids,
TOROs, project reviews, CAPA plans, SOW, etc.) are filed in the appropriate sections of the
study TMF or a designated secure repository
